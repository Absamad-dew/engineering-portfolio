# Earlier business automation: enquiries, production preparation and customer feedback

I developed and introduced operational tools for **three related businesses with around 15 people in total**. The work covered customer enquiries, CRM, production-file preparation and checking customer artwork. I worked with the manager and business lead, built the tools, explained how to use them and revised them around the actual workflow.

## 1. Customer enquiries connected to pricing and CRM

**Task and users.** Handle incoming marketplace enquiries without constantly interrupting other work, while keeping the customer's terms and order context available. This was a customer-operations workflow used in my business work, with the manager and business lead involved in the wider operating process.

**Before.** I watched incoming conversations and wrote replies manually, using message templates. Replying, checking terms, calculating a price and keeping order information together required separate attention.

**What I built.** I organized roughly 10,000 of my own historical messages into cases used to prepare responses to new enquiries in my style. The workflow used the **existing price calculator**, customer-specific discounts and terms, and transferred conversation and order context into the CRM. It also produced notifications. Proposed actions came back to me to confirm, reject or edit.

**My role and result.** I defined the response logic and examples, connected the workflow and retained the commercial approval decision. My operating estimate was that initial response delay fell from **up to an hour to around two minutes**. I spent less time keeping the inbox in focus and observed more orders. Follow-up conversations in the separate messenger channel remained manual.

## 2. Prepared vectors through to a cutting plan and material records

**Task and users.** Make production preparation a connected workflow, from approved artwork to a layout and file that could continue into ArtCAM. The work served production preparation, with human review and the later operator step retained.

**Before.** Composition, spacing, additional vectors and trimming contours were prepared manually before the result was transferred into ArtCAM. Sheet material also had to be entered into the system.

**What I built.** I implemented an application that composed prepared layouts, applied spacing and added the required vectors and contours. It could place the work on existing sheets and generate a cutting plan. New sheets were entered into the material records, and **offcuts remaining after cutting were recorded automatically**, avoiding repeat manual entry.

**My role and result.** I set the technical rules and built the application, keeping a check of the prepared result. The output continued into ArtCAM, where processing paths were formed using a template. My approximate comparison for this preparation process was **about two hours to 40 minutes**; it was a workflow estimate, not a measured time per order or machine cycle. The concrete operational change was a connected preparation sequence and automatic accounting for the remaining offcuts.

## 3. Artwork checks that produced actionable customer feedback

**Task and users.** Help customers understand whether their artwork met the technical requirements and what needed correcting. The workflow served both the people checking the file and the customer receiving the feedback.

**Before.** Checking a layout and explaining its problems to the customer were manual work. Each file needed both a technical assessment and an understandable response.

**What I built.** I translated production requirements into validation logic. The checker marked nonconforming areas, explained the required changes and generated a **ready-to-send PDF report**. The business work also included a customer-facing tool for uploading artwork, identifying errors and necessary corrections, and calculating the price.

**My role and result.** I defined the checks and implemented the tools. Checking and feedback became connected outputs rather than two separate manual tasks. In my practice, the program produced the check/report in roughly 30 seconds. Customers recommended the customer-facing service to others, and I observed additional orders and returning customers.

## Getting the tools used

One adoption challenge was changing a process that employees already considered workable. I personally showed how the new workflow completed the task with fewer actions, returned to the demonstration and addressed the reluctance to change. After a couple of approaches, colleagues began using it.

That experience made adoption part of delivery: the tool had to fit the work, be understandable to the people using it and make the practical improvement visible. The recorded acceptance standard was the quality of my own manual work: the automated result had to be at least as good.

**Evidence scope.** These are retrospective accounts of my earlier business practice. Timings are approximate working estimates, not a newly run benchmark; per-tool user counts and audited financial impact are not available. Business records, messages and application source remain private. [Aster](aster.md) is presented separately as a later product case.

Разрабатывал и внедрял инструменты для трёх связанных компаний, около 15 человек суммарно. Согласовывал задачи с менеджером и руководителем, реализовывал решения, объяснял работу и дорабатывал по обратной связи. Численность группы не означает, что каждым инструментом пользовались все 15 человек.

**Обращения и CRM.** Раньше вручную следил за перепиской и отвечал по шаблонам. Связал контекст собственных сообщений, условия клиента, существующий калькулятор и CRM. Система готовила ответ и действие, которое я подтверждал, отклонял или редактировал. **Подготовка производства.** Разработал приложение для композиции подготовленных макетов, зазоров, дополнительных векторов, контуров и карты раскроя. Обрезки после раскроя автоматически попадали в учёт. Файл передавался в ArtCAM; проверка и операторский этап сохранялись. Оценка для описанного процесса — около двух часов до 40 минут, не норма на заказ или машинный цикл.

**Проверка макетов.** Перевёл технические требования в логику проверки, отметку несоответствий и готовый PDF для заказчика. Также сделал клиентский инструмент загрузки, проверки и расчёта стоимости. Из практики программа готовила проверку/отчёт примерно за 30 секунд. Клиенты рекомендовали сервис, появлялись дополнительные заказы и постоянные клиенты; проценты и выручка не приписываются.

**Внедрение.** Сотрудники не сразу хотели менять работающий процесс. Лично показывал ту же задачу с меньшим числом действий; после нескольких подходов инструмент начали использовать. Критерием готовности было качество моей собственной ручной работы.

Это прежняя работа в бизнесе, отдельно от нынешней демонстрации Aster. Данные, переписка и исходники закрыты; оценки времени не являются бенчмарком.
